Caption (suggested): Study workflow. (1) Monthly data, 2016–2025: national lime production (MIDAGRI–SISAGRI (accessed [____])), NASA POWER climate, INDECI disaster records and agricultural news from Agraria.pe, scored with the pysentimiento RoBERTuito sentiment model (monthly mean sentiment and number of news items). (2) In each window, predictors enter with lags 1, 3 and 6 months; the scaler and the geographic weights are fitted on the training data only (up to December of Y−2), and the shock threshold is computed with the history up to the forecast origin (December of Y−1). (3) Rolling-origin evaluation with the origin in December of Y−1: training up to December of Y−2, validation in year Y−1 and test in year Y, for test years 2018–2025. In 2018–2020 only Naive, Seasonal Naive and Chronos are run (trained models need at least 36 training months). The model ranking uses 2021–2025; 2023–2025 is a pre-registered sensitivity set. (4) 10 models: Naive, Seasonal Naive, Chronos, SARIMA, SARIMA-sel, Ridge, ElasticNet, XGB-37, GC3 and GE. XGB-37, GC3 and GE are run with 10 seeds (0–9), and their monthly absolute errors are averaged over seeds. XGB-43 (dashed) is an exploratory, post hoc model outside the ranking. (5) MAE over all, shock and normal months; Δs = (MAE_shock − MAE)/MAE × 100; paired bootstrap 95% confidence intervals (months resampled within windows, B = 10,000); Diebold–Mariano tests with the Harvey–Leybourne–Newbold correction. The Holm adjustment is exploratory. Shock month (main definition): month-on-month change |y_t − y_(t−1)|/y_(t−1) above the 75th percentile of the changes observed up to the forecast origin; the 50th, 60th and 90th percentiles and the year-on-year change are reported as sensitivity.
